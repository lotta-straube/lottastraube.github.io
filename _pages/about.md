I am a doctoral researcher specializing in perception, empirical aesthetics, and sustainable product design.

My research investigates how consumers perceive and evaluate sustainable and circular products, with a particular interest in intercultural differences and their implications for design practice.

Before entering academia, I gained eight years of experience in the textile industry. My current work combines design research, experimental methods, and sustainability to support the development of products that are both environmentally responsible and attractive to users.
